Questions

Frequently asked.

Is the estimator figure binding?

No — it's a formal ballpark based on your inputs. A binding quotation is issued after a free site survey and soil test.

What does "Design + Build" include?

Everything from architectural drawings and approvals to on‑ground execution, material procurement and final handover under a single fixed‑price contract.

Do you work outside Gurugram / NCR?

Yes — BuildMart currently operates across 42+ cities including Bengaluru, Pune, Chennai, Nagpur and Surat, with regional site offices being added regularly.

What warranty do you offer?

All Design + Build contracts carry a 5‑year structural warranty and a 1‑year defect‑liability period on finishes.
